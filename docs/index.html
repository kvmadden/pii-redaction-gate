      What this does NOT do
      This prototype is not validated de-identification. A clean result means the checks found nothing left to decide. It does not mean the text is free of identifiers. Each result lists what was read, which checks ran, and the known gaps no check can see.
      Ages younger than 90 are kept on purpose. A lowercase name that is also an ordinary word, such as “grace”, can remain unless a title, relation word, greeting or person verb marks it. A facility named with a clinical word, such as Valley Hospital, goes to review instead of being removed.
      No OCR, fax image parsing, EDI ingestion, clinical interpretation, coverage decision or refill authorization. These examples are already transcribed English text. No clinical outcome or reviewer time savings have been measured.
      All input is synthetic. Rules run locally without model or vendor transfer. Drafts stay in page memory. Reviews store metadata locally, without authentication, cross-user isolation or tamper protection. Use the clear action on a shared browser. A flag does not contact a reviewer or automatically change detection.
