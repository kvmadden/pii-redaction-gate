MICROSCOPIC
All eight sites show distorted crypt architecture, basal plasmacytosis,
and crypt shortening, in keeping with chronic colitis. The inflamation
is mild and patchy, with cryptitis in the sigmoid and rectal samples
and little activity in the right colon. No granulomas are seen. No
dysplasia and no carcinoma are identified in the tissue examined. The
findings are consistant with quiescent to mildly active chronic
ulcerative colitis. A seperate comment on the requisition asks that
dysplasia be excluded, and it is excluded here.

The right-colon samples show Paneth cell metaplasia distal to the point
where those cells are normally expected, which supports chronicity.
Mucin preservation is good. No viral cytopathic effect is present.

DIAGNOSIS
Colon, cecum through rectum, biopsies: chronic colitis, mildly active
in the distal samples, inactive in the proximal samples. Negative for
dysplasia and negative for carcinoma.

The degree of activity fits a patient who has improved on steroids but
is not fully healed. Correlation with the endoscopic score, which was
not included on this requisition, is suggested. Results may be
telephoned to the number in the header.

COMMENT
The chronic changes are present in every jar, so this is read as
extensive rather than limited colitis. Activity, scored simply as
cryptitis without crypt abscesses, is confined to the two most distal
jars. That pattern often tracks the endoscopic appearance, although
no score was written on the bottle labels.

A cytomegalovirus immunostain was reviewed on the rectal block and is
negative, which matters because she is still on daily prednisone. No
amoeba, no granuloma, and no basal giant cells are seen. The tissue
is well oriented on most levels. One cecal fragment is superficial and
includes only the upper half of the mucosa, so a deep basal
lymphoplasmacytic band cannot be judged in that single jar; the other
seven jars do show the band.
