known ischemic cardiomyopathy, reduced ejection fraction, and baseline atrial
fibrillation, provoked primarily by dietary noncompliance and volume overload.
1. Volume Overload / Decongestion:
- Continue aggressive intravenous diuresis with furosemide 80 mg IV BID.
- Maintain strict intake and output recording and daily standing weights.
- Goal net fluid removal of 1.5 to 2.0 liters over the next 24 hours.
- Monitor basic metabolic panel every 12 hours during active titration.
2. Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy:

  euvolumic state is approached and renal function stabilizes.
- Continue carvedilol 12.5 mg PO BID; heart rate is appropriately controlled.
- Continue spironolactone 25 mg PO daily with close monitoring of potassium.
3. Anticoagulation & Comorbidities:
- Continue apixaban 5 mg PO BID for systemic thromboembolism prophylaxis.
- Maintain methimazole 5 mg PO daily for Graves disease.
- Complete final day of prednisone taper (10 mg PO daily) then discontinue.
