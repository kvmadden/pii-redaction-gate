MEDICATION RECONCILIATION

Performed by the covering pharmacist after the patient returned from an
inpatient stay. Records from Outside Hospital were used to rebuild the home
list. The patient is a 72-year-old woman. Her daughter helped with the
bottle count and confirmed which inhalers are actually in the house.

Reason for reconciliation
She was treated for community-acquired pneumonia together with an acute
exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Antibiotics have
been finished and are not continued. A steroid taper was started for the
lung flare and must be dispensed as a short course, not as a chronic
refill. She also lives with Parkinson disease, diagnosed in 2019, and with
type 2 diabetes mellitus since 2021, hypertension, and painful distal
sensory neuropathy in both feet. Recently she stumbled on a rug, did not
hit her head, and did not lose consciousness.

While swelling of the ankles was present during the stay, the Wells score
for deep vein thrombosis was low. Duplex ultrasound of both legs showed no
clot, and anticoagulation was not started. A chest radiograph showed a
focal infiltrate judged consistent with pneumonia. Repeat imaging was not
required once fever resolved and oxygen saturation recovered. Sputum
culture was not obtained.

The prednisone supply will be 10 mg tablets. She takes four tablets daily,
then three, then two, then one, each block lasting four days, which is the
same 40/30/20/10 mg taper. That is forty tablets in all. She should not
lengthen the taper on her own.

Gabapentin was tried previously at 300 mg PO TID and was stopped for
sedation. That trial is the clinical basis for requesting Lyrica. Brand
Lyrica needs prior authorization from the health plan. Pregabalin at the
same dose is the preferred alternative if the plan denies the brand. Her
daughter understands they are the same drug.

Counseling and holds
Sick-day plan: hold empagliflozin and metformin if she cannot eat or
drink, because of ketoacidosis and lactic acidosis risk, and restart when
intake is normal. Hold lisinopril if she has vomiting or marked
lightheadedness, then call the clinic. Do not start metoclopramide,
haloperidol, or a high-dose phenothiazine, because each can worsen
Parkinson disease. Urine may turn brown on entacapone, and that change is
not blood. Separate carbidopa-levodopa from a heavy protein meal when her
stomach allows it. Prednisone will raise glucose, so fingerstick checks
should increase during the taper, and Diabetes Center should see her if
readings stay high after the taper ends. A Pain Clinic referral is in
place if burning pain in the feet does not ease on pregabalin once the
steroid course is done. Inhaler technique was taught back correctly for
both the rescue inhaler and the daily anticholinergic inhaler. She should
rise slowly to limit levodopa-related dizziness. Acetaminophen remains the
analgesic. Ibuprofen was discouraged while blood pressure is being
watched. The bowel regimen is scheduled docusate, with senna only if she
has no stool for two days. A thirty-day supply of the chronic medicines
was queued, and the steroid quantity was limited to the taper. The
prescriber was asked to sign the prior authorization if the plan will not
accept pregabalin.
