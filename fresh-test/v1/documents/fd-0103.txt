CLINICAL CASE MANAGEMENT AND REFERRAL COORDINATION SUMMARY
SERVICE COORDINATION MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: COMPLEX CARE TRANSITION AND PHARMACY BENEFIT REVIEW

This administrative summary outlines the comprehensive clinical status,
treatment timeline, and ongoing therapeutic regimen for the patient, a
54-year-old female evaluated for treatment-refractory neuropathic pain
and cranial neuropathy. Diagnostic records transferred from Outside
Hospital corroborate a clinical onset dating back to an initial viral
syndrome diagnosed in 2021, which subsequently manifested as acute
right-sided facial paresis consistent with Bell palsy. Although facial
motor function demonstrated substantial recovery following an initial
course of oral corticosteroids, the patient developed persistent
secondary trigeminal and post-auricular dysesthesias accompanied by
autonomic dysregulation.

Current vital signs obtained during the most recent comprehensive
evaluation demonstrate clinical stability: blood pressure 128/76 mmHg,
heart rate 74 bpm, respiratory rate 16 breaths/min, body temperature
98.4 F, and oxygen saturation 99% on ambient air. Recent laboratory
panels show stable baseline organ function: serum creatinine 0.88 mg/dL,
blood urea nitrogen 14 mg/dL, potassium 4.2 mEq/L, sodium 140 mEq/L,
fasting plasma glucose 96 mg/dL, hemoglobin A1c 5.4%, and white blood
cell count 5.8 x10^3/uL with normal differential. Diagnostic imaging,
including contrast-enhanced magnetic resonance imaging of the brain and
internal auditory canal, demonstrated no structural lesions, vascular
compression, or demyelinating plaques.

An active multidisciplinary treatment plan has been established across
several clinical services. The patient has completed an initial intake
evaluation with Pain Clinic for neuromodulatory strategies and targeted
cranial nerve blocks. A concurrent referral to Cardiology was initiated
to evaluate episodic orthostatic lightheadedness prior to escalating
sympatholytic pharmacotherapy. Ongoing wound monitoring through Wound
Care remains on standby following minor cutaneous breakdown secondary
to adhesive patches.

Medication reconciliation reflects the current active pharmacotherapy:
- Lyrica 75 mg PO BID, titrated from 50 mg PO BID for neuropathic pain
- gabapentin 300 mg PO TID PRN for breakthrough hyperalgesia
- duloxetine 30 mg PO daily
- artificial tears 1 to 2 drops in the right eye q4h PRN
- prednisone 40/30/20/10 mg oral taper prescribed as a step-down burst
  over twelve consecutive days during the most recent acute exacerbation

To ensure continuous coverage and avoid gaps in pharmacy fulfillment,
the health plan has authorized ongoing maintenance therapy under the
chronic care management protocol. The covering pharmacist has reviewed
the combination of gabapentinoids and confirmed that clinical dosing
remains within safe therapeutic parameters. Follow-up consultations
are recommended every 2/3 weeks during the active dose titration phase.
The patient and her adult daughter have received detailed counseling
regarding medication administration schedules, potential somnolence,
and red-flag neurological symptoms that would warrant emergency medical
evaluation.
