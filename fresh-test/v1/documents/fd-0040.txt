PROCEDURE TECHNIQUE AND FINDINGS:
Prior to needle placement, informed written consent was reviewed with Mrs. Vong
and her daughter Patience, assisted by medical interpreter. Time-out verified
patient identity, procedure site, and anticoagulation hold (apixaban held 48h).

Diagnostic gray-scale and power Doppler sonography of the neck was performed.
The thyroid gland is markedly enlarged and multinodular.
Right thyroid lobe measures 6.2 x 3.1 x 2.9 cm.
Left thyroid lobe measures 5.4 x 2.4 x 2.2 cm.
The thyroid isthmus is thickened at 0.8 cm.

In the mid-to-lower pole of the right thyroid lobe, there is a dominant, solid,
hypoechoic nodule measuring 2.8 x 2.1 x 1.9 cm. The nodule displays irregular
margins, wider-than-tall orientation, and punctate echogenic foci suspicious
for microcalcifications (ACR TI-RADS 5, 7 points). Color Doppler interrogation
demonstrated peripheral and internal chaotic flow signals within the nodule.
Surrounding parenchyma shows patchy vascularity without discrete retrosternal
extension. Visualized jugular and carotid vessels were patent.

Following sterile preparation with chlorhexidine and local anesthesia using 4 mL
of 1% lidocaine, a 25-gauge needle attached to a 10 mL syringe was advanced into
the dominant right thyroid nodule under continuous real-time ultrasound guidance.
Three separate passes were performed. Adequate colloid and cellular material
were obtained. On-site cytotechnologist confirmed adequate cellularity. No
immediate hematoma or airway compromise occurred. Pressure dressing applied.
