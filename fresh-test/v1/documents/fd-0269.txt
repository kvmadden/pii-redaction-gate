Thank you for the detail, and for saying the arms and speech are normal. That history fits Bell palsy more than a brain event, as long as the weakness stays in the face. We still want a same-day exam if the face droop spreads, if you get a severe headache, or if an arm or a leg becomes weak.

Medications: prednisone 50 mg PO daily for 10 days, then stop. Do not taper this short course on your own and do not stretch it. Valacyclovir 1 g PO TID for 7 days.
