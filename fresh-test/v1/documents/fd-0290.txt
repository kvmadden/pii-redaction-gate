Assessment and plan
1. Volume overload on known HFrEF. The extra salt at a birthday lunch and the two missed diuretic doses are enough to explain the gain. Furosemide will increase to 40 mg by mouth twice daily for five days, then return to daily dosing if the morning weight is back at baseline. A basic metabolic panel is due in one week. She should call for a gain over 2 kg, dyspnea at rest, or new dizziness.
2. Chronic kidney disease, stage 3b. Creatinine is mildly above her usual number. Lisinopril stays on for now. Hold it if she is lightheaded or if the repeat creatinine climbs by more than 30 percent. No NSAIDs. A seperate renal clinic referral is not needed if the repeat panel is stable.
3. Contusion after a low-speed collision. History and exam do not suggest fracture or head injury, so radiographs are deferred. Acetaminophen is adequate. Her son will move the door mat and the boots so the path from the car is clear.
4. Hypothyroidism. TSH is in range on levothyroxine 88 mcg, and the dose stays the same.
