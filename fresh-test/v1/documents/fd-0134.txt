CT PULMONARY ANGIOGRAM

Patient: the patient
Sex: female
Age: 67 years
Ordering service: Diabetes Center
Comparison: outside imaging from 2019, brought over from Outside Hospital

INDICATION
67-year-old woman with dyspnea and a Wells score of 6. Her daughter supplied the medication list. The request came through Diabetes Center on the day of a glucose check.

TECHNIQUE
CT pulmonary angiography was performed from the lung apices through the upper abdomen after injection of 80 mL of iohexol 350 mg I/mL at 4.5 mL/sec. Images were reconstructed at 1 mm.

VITALS AND LABS
Blood pressure 148/86 mmHg. Heart rate 104 beats per minute. Respiratory rate 22 breaths per minute. Oxygen saturation 93 percent on room air. Temperature 37.1 C. D-dimer 1.84 mg/L FEU. Creatinine 1.1 mg/dL. Glucose 214 mg/dL. Hemoglobin A1c 8.4 percent. BNP 88 pg/mL.

MEDICATIONS
Lyrica (pregabalin) 75 mg PO BID. Metformin 1000 mg PO BID. Insulin glargine 18 units SUBQ at bedtime. Apixaban had not been started before the scan.

FINDINGS
The main, lobar, segmental, and subsegmental pulmonary arteries opacify without a filling defect. There is no evidance of acute pulmonary embolism. The right ventricle is not enlarged. No pleural effusion is seen. Mild dependant atelectasis is present at the bases.

IMPRESSION
1. No pulmonary embolus. The Wells score remains a clinical score and is not changed by this negative study.
2. Glycemic control is outside the scope of this report. Follow-up stays with Diabetes Center. Comparison with the 2019 images showed no new central embolus, and a seperate note is on file.

End of report.
