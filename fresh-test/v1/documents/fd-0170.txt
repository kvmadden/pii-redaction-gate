MICROSCOPIC EXAMINATION:
Sections of Mr. Yurtsever's prostate core biopsies were examined under
standard light microscopy following hematoxylin and eosin staining.

Parts A through E (Right lobe cores):
Cores show benign prostatic glandular tissue and fibromuscular stroma.
No acute or chronic prostatitis is identified. No atypia or malignancy.

Part F (Left mid gland peripheral zone):
Infiltrating small, crowded, distinct and well-formed neoplastic glands
infiltrating between benign acini. The neoplastic cells display nuclear
enlargement, prominent nucleoli, and absence of basal cell layer,
confirmed by immunohistochemical cocktail (p63 and high-molecular-weight
cytokeratin negative; AMACR/racemase positive). Appropriate positive and
negative external controls reacted as expected.
Gleason pattern: 3 + 4 = 7 (Grade Group 2).
Tumor involves approximately 35% of the core length (5 mm of 14 mm).
No perineural invasion is identified in this part.

Part G (Left apex):
Adenocarcinoma, acinar type.
Gleason pattern: 3 + 3 = 6 (Grade Group 1).
Tumor involves approximately 15% of the core length.

Parts H through L (Remaining left base and lateral cores):
Benign prostatic hypertrophy with focal stromal hyperplasia.
No evidence of extraprostatic extension or lymphovascular invasion.

FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
Acinar adenocarcinoma of the prostate, involving left mid gland and
left apex (2 of 12 cores positive).
Primary Gleason score: Gleason score 3 + 4 = 7, Grade Group 2.
Secondary Gleason pattern 4 comprises approximately 10% of tumor volume.
Surgical margins cannot be assessed on needle core biopsy.
