In March 2026, a 56‐year‐old Moroccan man, who had been living in Italy for several years, presented to the Emergency Department with persistent diffuse abdominal pain, predominantly localized to the mesogastric region, associated with constipation and clinical features suggestive of subacute intestinal obstruction. The patient was afebrile and denied urinary symptoms, recent travel abroad, ingestion of raw or poorly preserved food, or episodes of vomiting.
