Because bowel necrosis or perforation was considered unlikely, endoscopic reduction was performed. The volvulus was easily reduced without endoscopic evidence of mucosal ischemia. After reduction, abdominal pain and distension promptly improved. Until surgery, nutritional support consisted of parenteral nutrition and an oral liquid nutritional formula. Elective laparoscopic ileocecal resection was performed on hospital day 9, and the patient was discharged on postoperative day 14.
