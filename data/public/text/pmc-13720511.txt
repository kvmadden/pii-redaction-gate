Although the hemorrhage spared the fovea—making conservative observation a viable alternative—the patient experienced severe anxiety regarding the dense inferior visual field defect. Being in her third trimester of pregnancy, she felt the scotoma was disorienting and significantly increased her risk of falls, particularly when navigating stairs. Following comprehensive counseling on the risks and benefits of both observation and laser intervention, the patient opted for Nd:YAG laser hyaloidotomy to achieve rapid visual rehabilitation and alleviate her safety concerns.
