A 44-year-old Han woman with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status (ECOG PS) score of 1 was admitted to our hospital in August 2022. The patient was initially considered with localized epithelioid low-grade spindle cell tumors and underwent systemic chemotherapy with IA at another hospital in May 2022. Unfortunately, chemotherapy was discontinued due to severe myelosuppression (grade 4 leukopenia, grade 4 neutropenia, grade 2 anemia) after one cycle. Subsequent CT imaging in August 2022 indicated disease progression. The patient then received anlotinib (12 mg orally once daily) until September 2022, discontinued due to vaginal hemorrhage.
