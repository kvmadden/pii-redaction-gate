On admission, the patient appeared acutely ill and clinically dehydrated. He remained conscious but had cognitive slowing, impaired orientation, and dysarthria. Vital signs revealed a blood pressure of 119/88 mmHg, pulse rate of 126 beats/min, respiratory rate of 19 breaths/min, and oxygen saturation of 93% on room air. His height was 185 cm and weight was 84 kg, corresponding to a body mass index of 24.5 kg/m2. Physical examination demonstrated non‐pitting edema of the left lower extremity with reduced muscle strength (2/5), primarily attributable to pain. Peripheral pulses were preserved.
